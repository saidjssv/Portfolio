About Me

Hi, I'm Said. I'm from Mexico and have a passion for technology and programming.

I started programming when I was 13, mostly out of curiosity and a need to understand how things work.

My first real project was a Discord bot called Nebulosity, built with JavaScript, where I learned by breaking things and fixing them again.

Later, I represented my school at the Mexican Informatics Olympiad (OMI), where I was introduced to C++ and strengthened my problem-solving logic and algorithmic thinking.

Currently, I'm 17 years old andfocused on web development while exploring cybersecurity fundamentals. I enjoy learning by building, refactoring, and questioning how systems behave under the hood.
